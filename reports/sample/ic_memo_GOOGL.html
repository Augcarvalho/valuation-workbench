1 · Situation Overview

Alphabet Inc. posted 21.8% latest-quarter revenue growth versus the prior-year quarter, with a EBITDA margin of 38.2%. Cash conversion is adequate at 40.0% of EBITDA, and the balance sheet carries 0.2x net leverage. Alphabet is a scaled digital platform with one of the highest-quality advertising franchises globally, a growing cloud business, and a balance sheet that gives management unusual strategic flexibility. The investment question is whether AI disruption compresses search economics or reinforces the company's distribution, data, and infrastructure advantages.

Search and YouTube remain scaled, cash-generative advertising assets.
Google Cloud can keep improving margins as utilization and enterprise mix mature.
Net cash and buybacks provide downside support while the company invests through the AI transition.

4 · Variant Perception

The market often frames Alphabet as an AI-disruption risk. The variant view is that AI raises capex intensity but also deepens the value of distribution, proprietary data, cloud infrastructure, and advertiser relationships.

5 · Key Debate
